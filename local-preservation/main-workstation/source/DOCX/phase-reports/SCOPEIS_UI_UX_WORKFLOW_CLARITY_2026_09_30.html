What changed

Find the right workspace

Navigation groups daily work, people, client work, overview and administration. Icons, selected states and the account menu are consistent across desktop and mobile.

See what needs attention

Dashboard priorities follow the signed-in role. Review queues lead Super Admin to decisions; employees start with their upcoming work and personal information.

Complete one task at a time

Creation, editing, requests and review actions open titled dialogs. Errors remain with the form; confirmations survive actions that remove their own card.

Follow the planning sequence

Select a month directly, follow Plan → Review → Published, and open coverage from the assignment with the employee, project, location and time already shown.

Read records before editing

Client, project, location, employee and account screens show their records and relationships first. Supporting notes, contacts and maintenance controls no longer dominate the page.

Understand supporting information

Leave balances, report groups, notification filters, profile summaries and evidence sections use clearer hierarchy, practical labels and visible next actions.
