# Phase 6 Skills and Operational Capabilities Decisions

Phase 6 records controlled qualification facts and explains missing recorded skills. It does not determine coverage, safety eligibility, performance, certification status, or replacement candidates.

- The Phase 2 `skills` catalogue and `employee_skills` association remain the sole catalogue and employee-skill stores. Super Admin alone creates, renames, activates/reactivates, and archives catalogue skills, and alone records or archives employee-skill associations. Archiving is non-destructive: historical employee and requirement references remain readable, while archived skills cannot be newly assigned or newly required.
- A qualification fact is only an active recorded employee-skill association. Legacy notes, experience, proficiency, certification references, verification state, and coverage-eligibility fields remain preserved but have no Phase 6 filtering, warning, or schedule effect.
- Existing versioned `staffing_requirements` are the independent Client, Project, and Location required-skill associations. Their inherited `requiredEmployeeCount` is preserved for future coverage work but is not interpreted in Phase 6. A new versioned, archival `assignment_skill_requirements` association adds an individual Draft assignment source.
- The effective requirement set is the de-duplicated union of active requirement records on the assignment’s linked Client, Project, Location, and its active assignment-specific records. Each result retains all contributing sources. Nothing is inferred from designation, hierarchy, Account Manager, employee association, staffing counts, or arrangement labels.
- Super Admin can manage all work requirements. Admin uses the established explicit Client/Project/Location schedule scope for operational records and Draft assignment requirements only. Operational scope never widens employee-skill visibility; Admin candidate results are separately limited to explicit active `TEAM` grants. Employees have no management controls and can read only their own recorded skills.
- A server-calculated missing-skill warning means only that an assignment’s employee lacks one or more effective required recorded skill associations. During Super Admin review it names the employee, assignment date, missing skill, and contributing source(s). It is non-blocking, produces no special override or audit event, and does not mutate Draft, Proposed, or Published schedules. Normal Phase 4 audit and lifecycle behavior remains intact.
- Phase 5 approved-leave and Phase 4 overlap controls remain independent scheduling integrity rules. Phase 6 introduces no notifications and no availability, coverage, ranking, replacement, map, ticket, payroll, attendance, travel, or production behavior.
