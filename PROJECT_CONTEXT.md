# Project Context

ScopeIs Team Management System is a documentation-first workforce planning product for an internal engineering team of approximately 15-20 people. It will help managers understand skills, assignments, availability, clients, projects, locations, leave, coverage, and replacement options across future weeks and months.

All employees are internal. Labels such as "Outsourced to Client" describe an assignment arrangement, never an employment type and never a source of business logic. Actual dates, times, assignments, skills, locations, approved leave, and coverage rules determine availability and conflicts.

The confirmed system roles are Super Admin, Admin, and Employee; roles are independent of job designations. Only the Super Admin/team manager publishes schedules and approves or rejects leave. Admins may draft schedules and request replacements within their scope, but do not publish schedules or advise on leave decisions. Employees see only published schedules. The management map is static planning based on stored data, not GPS or live tracking.

Phase 9 — Certifications, CVs, portfolios, and files is `COMPLETED`. Employees maintain certifications, portfolio links and files, project examples, one active CV, and supporting capability documents from `/profile`; submissions save immediately without an approval gate, flag their own new/updated state, and transactionally notify active Super Admins, who may review, verify, remove verification, or reset from the employee detail context. Phase 10 — notes, discussions, the complete notification centre, and the audit interface — is `COMPLETED`. Shared Client, Project, and Location notes preserve the previous content as a transactional revision, keep editing author-only, and keep archive Super Admin-only with a retained reason; the product owner confirmed that these notes are readable and creatable only by authenticated users already authorized on the parent record, so Employees gain no Client, Project, Location, or shared-note access. Employee-management notes enforce private-to-author and shared-upward visibility with immutable content and non-enumerating refusals for the subject, peers, and out-of-scope Admins. Participant-only discussions are limited to replacement requests, with participants derived live from the request. The notification centre and the Super Admin audit history complete the central interfaces, and the approved Phase 9 evidence-integrity prerequisite resets review and verification provenance on a material owner edit. See the [Phase 10 report](DOCX/phase-reports/SCOPEIS_PHASE_10_NOTES_DISCUSSIONS_NOTIFICATION_CENTRE_AND_AUDIT_INTERFACE_R1.md).

Phase 11 — dashboards, reports, and exports — is `COMPLETED`. `/dashboard` is now a role-branched operational summary and `/reports` is a scope-enforced report index over thirteen registered metric contracts, each declaring its source of truth, grain, inclusion and exclusion rules, date interpretation, role policy, privacy class, ordering and empty-state behaviour. Authoritative staffing metrics read only the current Published schedule; the Draft and Proposed planning view is a separate `planning-unpublished` report labelled `PLANNING (unpublished)` on the page, heading, filename and every exported row, reachable by a scoped Admin only inside their current scope and never by an Employee. Availability terminology was removed in favour of a four-value conflict fact computed from active status, approved leave and Published overlap only. Exports stream bounded CSV, re-authorized per request, capped at 5,000 rows with refusal rather than truncation, protected against spreadsheet formula injection and audited with safe metadata only. No migration was required. See the [Phase 11 report](DOCX/phase-reports/SCOPEIS_PHASE_11_DASHBOARDS_REPORTS_AND_AUTHORIZED_EXPORTS_R1.md).

All database verification uses freshly created disposable loopback-only databases with fictional data, and no gate reads `.env.production`. An ordinary `npm run build` is not sanctioned while a local `.env.production` can be auto-loaded; `npm run build:safe` is the authoritative isolated build gate. The historical root prototype `prototype/full-frontend-r1/` is preserved reference work and sits outside the authoritative lint boundary; it must not be deleted, edited, formatted, migrated, or treated as application source.
